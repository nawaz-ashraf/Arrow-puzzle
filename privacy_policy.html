Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 1, 2026
This Privacy Policy explains how Arrow Escape ("we", "our", or "the app") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our mobile game. By playing Arrow Escape, you agree to this policy.

Information We Collect

Arrow Escape is a free, ad-supported game. We collect only the information necessary to operate the app and serve ads.

Information collected automatically:

  • Device type, OS version, and language
  • Approximate location (country/region level only)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks)
  • App usage statistics (levels completed, session duration)
  • Crash reports and performance data

Information we do NOT collect:

  • Your name, email address, or phone number
  • Precise GPS location
  • Photos, camera, or microphone data
  • Contacts or social media accounts
  • Payment information

Advertising & Third-Party SDKs

Arrow Escape uses Google AdMob to display ads. AdMob may collect and use data to serve personalized or non-personalized ads depending on your consent and applicable law.

You can opt out of personalized ads:

  • iOS: Settings → Privacy → Tracking → disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track"

The following third-party services may be integrated and have their own privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob — Ad serving
  • Firebase / Crashlytics — Crash reporting (if enabled)

How We Use Information

The data collected is used exclusively to:

  • Display relevant advertisements to keep the game free
  • Analyze app performance and fix bugs
  • Improve gameplay experience and difficulty balancing
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell your personal data to any third party.

Data Stored on Your Device

Arrow Escape stores the following data locally on your device only using SharedPreferences. This data never leaves your device:

  • Current level progress and highest level unlocked
  • Star ratings per level
  • Hint count and daily claim date
  • Login streak and daily reward status
  • Sound preference

Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Arrow Escape is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA)

Depending on your region, you may have the right to:

  • Access — Request a copy of data we hold about you
  • Deletion — Request deletion of your personal data
  • Correction — Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Opt-Out — Opt out of personalized advertising
  • Portability — Request your data in a portable format

To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

Data Retention

Game progress data is stored locally and retained until you uninstall the app or clear app data. Advertising data is subject to Google AdMob's retention policies, typically up to 63 days for standard advertising metrics.
